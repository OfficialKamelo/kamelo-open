Frequently asked questions

Is Kamelo free?

Yes. Kamelo is free for Chrome and Edge. There's no account and no payment, and Kamelo never asks for a password or payment. Only download it from the Chrome Web Store or Microsoft Edge Add-ons.

Which AI tools does it work with?

ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Copilot, Perplexity, Mistral, DeepSeek, Grok, Meta AI and more. It also works in AI features on other sites, such as "Help me write" in Gmail, Slack AI and Notion AI.

On any other site, open the Kamelo popup and turn on Mask every text field.

One exception: the Copilot panel built into Edge’s sidebar. It is part of the browser itself, and Microsoft doesn’t let any extension work there. Use Copilot in a normal tab at copilot.microsoft.com instead.

What does Kamelo protect?

Names, email addresses, phone numbers, addresses and dates of birth. Bank details such as IBANs from 87 countries, card numbers, ID numbers like SSN and BSN, passwords and API keys. You can also add your own terms, such as client or project names.

Does my data leave my device?

No. Kamelo makes no network requests of its own: no analytics, no tracking, no account. The link between placeholders and your real details is kept in a local vault in your browser. You can lock it with Touch ID or a password, and clear it any time.

Does Kamelo catch everything?

No, and we'd rather be honest about it. Kamelo can miss things, especially unusual names, and it doesn't filter uploaded files yet. Take a quick look before you send something very sensitive.

What about health, money or legal topics?

Some things can't be replaced by a placeholder, like your health, faith, money problems or a court case. When you share something personal about these, Kamelo shows a short notice suggesting a temporary chat. General questions don't trigger it.

Kamelo masked something it shouldn't have.

Click the word in the notice to keep it visible. It goes on your Never protect list, and you can undo it straight away.

How do I turn Kamelo off?

Click the Kamelo icon in your browser's toolbar and use the switch in the popup. The popup also shows whether Kamelo is protecting the site you're on.
