Get Kamelo

AI doesn't need to know everything.

Kamelo's mission is to let everyone use AI without giving away their personal data.

Every day, people paste emails, letters, contracts and questions about money and health into AI. Most of it contains someone's name, address or bank details. You shouldn't have to strip that out by hand, or stop using AI to stay private. Kamelo does it for you, on your own device, before anything is sent.

Kamelo comes from chameleon.

A chameleon blends into its surroundings and doesn't stand out. Your personal data should do the same.

What we believe

Your data stays with you

Everything happens on your device. No account, no servers, no tracking. Kamelo never contacts a server, not even ours.

Honest about limits

Kamelo can miss things, especially unusual names, and doesn't filter uploaded files yet. We'd rather tell you than promise "100% safe".

Calm, not scary

We explain what happens to your chats. We don't sell fear.

Made in the Netherlands

Kamelo is built in Amsterdam, under strict European privacy rules.

Free to start

The basics are free for Chrome and Edge, with no account and no paywall. Kamelo never asks for a password or payment.
