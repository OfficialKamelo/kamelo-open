Frequently asked questions

Will Kamelo stay free?

Yes. Everything Kamelo does for one person stays free, with no account and no ads. Teams pay for central setup and management.

When is Kamelo for teams available?

We're building it now with the offices on the waitlist. Join and we'll let you know first, and you can help decide what's in it.

Does Kamelo for teams send data to a server?

No. Detection and the vault stay on each computer, just like the free version. The team overview only shows counts, never what was typed.

Are prices with or without VAT?

Prices are per person per month, excluding VAT.
